This App allows users to take photos or select images from their device to perform face detection and age estimation locally on the device. The App may display ads using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

2. Data We Process

2.1 Images (Camera / Gallery)

Images are processed locally on your device for face detection and age estimation.

We do not collect, store, or transmit facial data to our servers.
